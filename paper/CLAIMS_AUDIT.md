VGAS supplies the selector and released checkpoints. The paper contributions are the shared-prefix implementation and system timing, the empirical early-window allocation study, and the evaluation of packed critic storage. All hardware measurements cited above use A100. See the [archive map](../docs/EXPERIMENT_ARCHIVE.md) for the corrected Object run. The earlier 1,200-episode wrapper batch always executed candidate zero and is excluded. README and workshop comparisons match the accepted evidence at their reported precision.
